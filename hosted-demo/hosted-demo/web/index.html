UNIT 5 / WEIGHTED SET COVER

The next ink earns its place.

For every unused ink, count the new colours it can cover within your tolerance. Pick the lowest cost ÷ new colours covered. Repeat until no more colours can be covered.

This is an approximation. For a feasible cover with positive costs, the greedy cost is bounded by Hₙ × optimum. The press limit is a separate check.

SELECTION / QUICKSELECT + MEDIAN OF MEDIANS

Find the shifts worth checking.

Choose randomized Quickselect or deterministic Median of Medians to find the top-k cutoff. Boundary ties follow source order. A binary max-heap then presents the chosen colours from largest to smallest shift.

Quickselect: expected O(n), worst O(n²). Median of Medians: worst O(n). Max-heap: O(k) build + O(k log k) to order the review queue. Both selection implementations use O(n) extra space. Randomized Quickselect also computes p90 in either mode.

BUILT FOR DESIGNERS

Keep the character of your palette.

Imagine a poster with eight colours. Could similar colours share one printing ink? OVERPRINT suggests a combination and shows what your poster could look like. You can change the settings until the result feels right.

Choose your colours, compare the before and after, check the biggest changes, then save the result. The detailed computer methods are available under How it works.

This version recolours the sample poster. It does not upload arbitrary artwork or predict exactly how a physical printer will behave.
